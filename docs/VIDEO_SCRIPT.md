## 1. Problem and track (0:00 to 1:00)

On screen: the editor or terminal at the repository root (no slide deck).

Say: a precedent can match your query perfectly and still be unsafe to cite, for two reasons. The statute changed: the BNS
replaced the IPC on 1 July 2024, numbers moved (murder, IPC 302, is BNS 103), numbers collided (BNS 302 is not murder) and some
provisions were omitted or are new; the offence date decides which code applies, so both are live. Or a later bench overruled,
doubted or criticised it. Free tools rank by text relevance only; paid tools show a flag. We use statutory continuity and judicial
treatment **inside the ranking function**, and we show the evidence. That is Track 6: a vertical search engine that uses the
domain's structure (zones, metadata, citations, authority). The output is treatment signals with evidence, not legal advice.
